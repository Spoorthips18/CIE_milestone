Problem Statement: 
A simple cash-flow management dashboard for small businesses. 

1.What product are you planning to build? 

A user-friendly cash flow management dashboard to allow small businesses to manage 
income and expenses, pending payments, and overall financial situation in one platform. 

2.What problem does it solve? 
While the owners of small businesses keep track of their finances, they are usually unsure 
whether they have sufficient cash flow and what financial challenges and risks lie ahead. 

3.Who are the target users? 
Small business entrepreneurs, store owners, freelancers, and small teams without their own 
financial managers or advanced accounting technologies.  

4.What is the main use case? 
The owner simply inputs his or her transactions and pending payments, and the dashboard 
displays cash flow position, possible risks, and simple tips on money management. 

5.What are the core features? 
Transaction logging, income/expense tracking, pending payments monitoring, cash flow 
reports, and warning notifications. 

6.What assumptions are you making about the users? 
We assume that our users possess basic information about their income and expenses but 
would prefer using a simple visual dashboard instead of sophisticated accounting tools. 

7.What will be included in your MVP? 
Manual transaction logging, income/expense classification, pending payment tracking, cash 
flow summaries, and fresh alerts. 

8.What will NOT be included in the MVP? 
Integration with bank accounts, transaction synchronization, tax preparation, payroll 
outsourcing, advanced bookkeeping, and investment management.
